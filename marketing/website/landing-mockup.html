§ 1 · Demonstrations

Three runs, start to finish.

Demo before vision. Here is what TeXRA actually does on a working paper, in the order most people try it.

Scene APolish, with proof

It is straightforward to see that We now show that the estimate (12) is sharp, and and and the proof basically follows by duality.

Run polish on a section. It rewrites for clarity and flow, then compiles a latexdiff PDF of every edit. Read it like a referee; accept or reject. correct does the same for typos while preserving your style, and latexFixer repairs broken compiles.

Scene BThe derivation check

∫ u dv = uv + − ∫ v du (15)
✗ review: sign flip in the second term of (15)
✓ wolfram: correction confirmed symbolically
→ fix proposed as a diff, waiting for approval

Ask review to verify a derivation. It checks soundness step by step, confirms suspect algebra with Wolfram tooling, and proposes the correction as a diff. research handles analytical derivations and numerics the same way.

texra chat is a full TUI: live progress, a focused subagent view, mid-chat /model switching, image paste. For scripts, texra run polish --input - pipes stdin and --print emits json or ndjson.

§ 4 · Referee questions

Questions a careful reviewer would ask.

Academics will probe an AI writing tool, and they should. We answer the way we would answer a referee: directly.

Q1Is using this academic misconduct?

TeXRA assists; the author stays in control and accountable. Every change is a reviewable diff you accept or reject, so the judgment in the paper remains yours. Agents like criticize, review, and verifyFix exist for rigor, not ghostwriting.

Follow your venue's and your institution's disclosure policies. That part is on you, and it should be.

Q2Will it hallucinate math?

Models err, so TeXRA is built around verification, not trust. review checks derivation soundness with Wolfram tooling, verifyFix runs a three-phase verify and fix loop, and every correction arrives as a diff you inspect. The author is the final check.

Q3Will it mangle my LaTeX, or my voice?

Edits never write directly to your files. They wait in versioned run storage as math-mode-safe latexdiff PDFs until you accept them. correct fixes typos while preserving your style, rejecting a change costs one click, and latexFixer repairs broken compiles, including broken DIF markup.

Q4What does it cost?

GitHub or Google sign-in includes free model access, with the monthly quota visible in Settings. The Researcher Access Program gives academics complimentary hosted access with enough budget to run the full orchestrator plus specialist roster. Power users bring their own API keys, stored encrypted, with automatic fallback when the free quota is exhausted.

Q5Why not just ChatGPT?

A browser tab cannot open your .tex files, compile them, or run latexdiff. TeXRA works in your editor and terminal with 56+ tools (Zotero, arXiv, Crossref, Wolfram, Lean diagnostics, git) and persistent, resumable run history across all three surfaces.

Honestly: if copy-paste still works for you, keep it.
