Here is my attempt at brainstorming ideas and writing the first draft of a 20,000 word novel about Real Madrid:

<brainstorming>

Plot ideas:
- The story of Real Madrid over multiple decades/generations, following key players and coaches who helped shape the club's legendary status 
- A fictionalized retelling of Real Madrid's most famous victories and defining moments through the eyes of players, coaches, fans
- The rise of a young academy prospect who dreams of leading Real Madrid to glory
- Power struggles and ego clashes behind the scenes between star players and coaches
- A scandal or controversy that rocks the club, testing the loyalty and integrity of those involved
- The Signning of Leo Messi  (using 2 chapters)
- The Signning of Mbappe to the football team (using 3 chapters)

Key characters:
- Alfredo Di Stefano - striker from the 1950s, helped define Real Madrid's playing style 
- Santiago Bernabéu - former player, coach and president who helped transform Real Madrid 
- Raúl González - the "eternal captain", embodiment of Real Madrid for over 15 years
- Cristiano Ronaldo - global superstar, club's all-time leading goalscorer
- Florentino Pérez - powerful club president who ushered in the Galácticos era
- Zinedine Zidane - iconic player who later coached the club to three straight UEFA Champions League titles
- Karim Benzema - longtime striker and current star player
- Leo Messi
- Karim Mbappe

Themes to explore:
- What does it mean to be a true "madridista"? The core values of Real Madrid 
- Handling the immense pressure and expectations that comes with playing for Real Madrid
- Balancing individual glory/ego with sacrificing for the good of the team
- The weight of upholding Real Madrid's winning tradition and legendary status
- How money, business and politics interface with the club's sporting ambitions
- Real Madrid as a symbol that unites people across Spain and the world 

Settings:
- Santiago Bernabéu Stadium - the iconic home of Real Madrid
- Ciudad Real Madrid - the club's training ground and academy facility 
- Madrid, Spain - capturing the history, culture and people of the city
- Other major European cities - for away matches and rivalries with other top clubs
- Hometowns and origin stories of key players in Latin America, Europe, etc.

</brainstorming>
