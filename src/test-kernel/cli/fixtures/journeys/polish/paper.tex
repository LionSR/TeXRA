\section{Setup}\label{sec:setup}
We consider a chain of N spins with nearest-neighbour coupling J... The ground state energy is given by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:energy}
  E_0 = -J \sum_{i=1}^{N} \sigma_i \sigma_{i+1}
\end{equation}
% We set $J=1$ for sufficient precision in our numerical analysis.
As shown in equation \ref{eq:energy}, the energy is extensive. The so-called "thermodynamic limit" is reached when N is large.
