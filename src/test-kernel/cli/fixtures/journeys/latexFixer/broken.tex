\section{Estimate}
Let $f$ be continuous on $[0,1]$. Then
\begin{equation}
  \left| \int_0^1 f(x)\,dx \right| \le \sup_{x} |f(x)|
\end{equation}
and for the rate we write
\begin{equation}
  r = \frac{1}{2 + \alhpa}
\end{equation}
where $\alpha > 0$ is fixed. The Cauchy--Schwarz inequality gives the bound
\begin{equation}
  \left( \int_0^1 fg \right)^2 \le \int_0^1 f^2 \int_0^1 g^2 .
\end{equation}
