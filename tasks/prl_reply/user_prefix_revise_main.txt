Explanation of documents:
1. Cover Letter: The original letter submitted with the paper, explaining its significance and suitability for the journal.
2. Main Paper: The full text of the submitted research paper, containing the core content and findings.
3. Appendix: Supplementary material that provides additional details, data, or explanations to support the main paper.
4. Editor Decision Letter: The letter from the journal editor, typically containing their initial decision and a summary of the referee reports.
5. Referee Report A: The detailed feedback and critique from the first referee.
6. Referee Report B: The detailed feedback and critique from the second referee.
7. Example Reply Letter: A sample response letter to guide the structure and tone of your reply.
8. Instruction: Specific guidelines or requirements for drafting the reply letter.
9. Draft Reply Letter: An initial version of the response to the referee reports, which you will review and improve.
