When polishing, use your own words, but keep the detail and structure of the drafted reply letter.

Explanation of documents:
1. Cover Letter: The original cover letter submitted with the paper.
2. Main Paper: The full text of the submitted paper, identified by {INPUT_FILE}.
3. Appendix: Supplementary information for the paper.
4. Editor Decision Letter: The full text of the editor's decision and comments.
5. Referee Report A: The full report from the first referee.
6. Referee Report B: The full report from the second referee.
7. Example Reply Letter: A sample reply letter for reference.
8. Draft Reply Letter: The initial draft of the reply letter that needs polishing.
